Getting started

Pick a picture, then point at any pixel.

Images are chosen only when you take an action. The app has no account and does not upload selected images to the developer’s servers.

1

Add an image

Take a photo with the camera, choose one from Photos, import a file, or use the Paste button for an image you copied.

2

Sample its color

Tap or drag over the image. The loupe and crosshair help you choose the exact source pixel.

3

Copy the value

Copy RGB, uppercase HEX, HSL, or the closest standard color name individually.

Common questions

A few useful details.

What does “exact” mean?

It means the selected source pixel converted to 8-bit sRGB. Screens, image profiles, lighting, printers, paint, and other physical materials can still look different.

What is a closest standard color?

It is the nearest descriptive match from a built-in standard color list. It is not a Pantone, paint, brand, or material guarantee.

How does the free allowance work?

Three unique images are free on each device. An image counts only once you pick a color from it, so opening the wrong photo and going back costs nothing — and you can sample as many colors as you like from each image. Re-opening an image you already used is always free. A device-only Keychain counter administers the allowance; it may not reset after reinstalling the app.

What does Pro Lifetime include?

Pro Lifetime is a one-time, non-recurring in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited imports. Open Settings in the app to restore a prior purchase.

How do I delete my saved content?

In the app’s Settings, choose Delete All Saved Images and Color History. This does not reset the device-only free-import counter.

I bought Pro. How do I use it on my other device?

Pro Lifetime is tied to the Apple Account that bought it, not to one device. Install the app on your other iPhone or iPad while signed in to that Apple Account, and Pro unlocks on its own — there is normally nothing to tap. If it ever shows as locked, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, which is also on the purchase screen. You are never charged twice: Apple blocks a repeat purchase of a one-time item and restores it instead.

Do my saved images and colors move to a new device?

No. Saved copies and color history are stored only on the device and are excluded from backups, so they are not restored onto a new iPhone or iPad. Only your Pro Lifetime purchase carries over. Your original photos are unaffected — they stay wherever you keep them.

Can someone in my family use my purchase?

Not in this version. Pro Lifetime is tied to the Apple Account that made the purchase, and Family Sharing is not enabled for it. A different Apple Account gets its own three free images and would need to purchase separately.
