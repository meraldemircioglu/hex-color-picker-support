Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-12
Developer: Meral Demircioğlu

Summary

Hex Color Picker is designed to work without an account. We do not operate image-storage servers, do not sell personal information, and do not use advertising or cross-app tracking.

Images and color history

Images you take with the camera or select from Photos, Files, or the Paste control are processed on your device. A photo you take inside the app is used only to sample a color from it; it is not added to your photo library and is never uploaded. Saved image copies and color history remain on your device. The app does not transmit selected images, sampled colors, or image metadata to Meral Demircioğlu, RevenueCat, or advertising providers.

The app removes EXIF and location metadata from copies it saves; it does not modify your original image. App-owned saved copies, thumbnails, and color history are excluded from device backups. The app itself does not upload selected images. Your original image’s source service, such as Photos, Files, or iCloud, may separately store, synchronize, or back up the original under its own settings and policies.

When you tap a copy button, the app writes the color value as text to your device’s system clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. Only the color text is copied, never the image. If you have Universal Clipboard enabled in your Apple settings, iOS may make that clipboard text available to your other Apple devices; that is an operating-system feature and the text does not pass through us.

Free-import allowance

The app stores a device-only counter and small set of image-content fingerprints in Apple Keychain to administer the three-image free allowance without charging a duplicate image twice. A fingerprint is a one-way hash of image pixels; it cannot be turned back into your image, and it never leaves the device. This is not an account or a profile, and we cannot access it remotely. Device changes, restores, operating-system behavior, or reinstalling the app may affect this local trial information.

Retention and deletion. This counter is kept for as long as the app remains installed, because its purpose is to make the free allowance a one-time allowance. It is stored only on your device and is not synchronized to iCloud. Deleting saved images and color history in the app’s Settings deliberately does not reset it. Because it is held in the device Keychain rather than by us, we cannot delete it on your behalf; removing the app and its Keychain data through iOS removes it. Buying Pro Lifetime makes the counter irrelevant — the app stops consulting it.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and determine whether Pro Lifetime is unlocked. RevenueCat may process a RevenueCat-generated anonymous app-user identifier, device and technical information, transaction receipts, and purchase history for purchase validation, entitlement delivery, fraud prevention, and purchase reporting.

We do not provide RevenueCat with names, email addresses, images, or custom account identifiers. We do not use advertising, behavioral analytics, IDFA, or cross-app tracking. RevenueCat’s transaction reporting is used for purchase management, not advertising or cross-app tracking.

Learn more in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Support requests

If you email us at mdappssupport@gmail.com, we receive the information you choose to include. Google (Gmail) processes those messages on our behalf. We retain support correspondence for six months, unless a longer period is required by law or needed to resolve an issue.

This website

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising tools, or contact forms. GitHub may process technical information, including visitor IP addresses, to host and secure this site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your choices and requests

You can delete saved images and color history in the app’s Settings, manage purchases through Apple, and contact mdappssupport@gmail.com about a support-related privacy request. Because images and color history are local-only, we cannot retrieve or delete them remotely. Deleting saved content does not reset the device-only free-import counter.

International processing and updates

Apple, RevenueCat, GitHub, and Google (Gmail) may process the purchase, technical, hosting, or support data described above in countries other than yours, subject to their policies and applicable safeguards. We may update this policy; changes will appear on this page with a revised effective date. For questions, contact mdappssupport@gmail.com.
